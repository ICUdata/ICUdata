## How to apply
1. Contact the ICUdata contact person at your institution.
2. Submit a research proposal to the ICUdata Scientific Committee.
3. After approval you get a MyDre account and are added to the ICUdata workspace (see `instructies/en/getting-started.md`).

The data are accessed in the MyDre research environment over standard protocols (HTTPS for the portal, RDP or SSH for the virtual machines),
with authentication and authorisation per user.
