# 1. Gaining access

The ICUdata database is hosted on the MyDRE platform by AnDREa, a protected research environment with minimal connection to the internet.

1. Ask the primary ICUdata contact person of your institution for access. This person can request a MyDRE account for researchers of their institution.
2. After approval, a MyDRE account is created for you, and you are added to the ICUdata research workspace.
3. If you want to use the data for research, send a research request to the Scientific Committee of ICUdata.

> **Note:** When you create a new password, do not use one created by a password manager. You will have to type it in the VM many times, and the VM has no internet connection and no copy-paste between your own computer and the VM.
