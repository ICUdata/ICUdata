# 1. Gaining access

## What MyDRE is

The ICUdata database is hosted on MyDRE, a platform by AnDREa that hosts data and virtual computers (VMs). The VMs contain our data. To stay GDPR compliant, MyDRE strongly limits connections to and from the outside:

- Data can only be taken out of MyDRE on request and with our permission; see [Getting results out of MyDRE](05-data-and-code.md#getting-results-out-of-mydre).
- You cannot copy and paste between your own PC and MyDRE.

## Who can get access

Access is only possible for researchers at hospitals that deliver data to ICUdata.

1. Ask the primary ICUdata contact person of your hospital for access. This person can request a MyDRE account for researchers of their hospital.
2. After approval, a MyDRE account is created for you, and you are added to the ICUdata research workspace.

## Browsing versus research

- **Browsing is allowed.** With your account you may look through the database to see which data it contains.
- **Using the data for analysis always requires a research request** to the Scientific Committee of ICUdata. Send it before you start an analysis.

> **Note:** When you create a new password, do not use one created by a password manager. You will have to type it in the VM many times, and the VM has no internet connection and no copy-paste between your own computer and the VM.
